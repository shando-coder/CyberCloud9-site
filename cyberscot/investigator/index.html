Signals in the Evidence

At the Clydebeat Festival in Port Clyde, someone saved a copy of the festival’s ticket booking list shortly after a failed sign-in. The festival organiser wants to know what happened. Can the records identify who exported it?

25–35 minutes · One browser · Three tasks

What is a ticket booking list?

When someone books tickets for Clydebeat, the festival’s booking system adds a row to a list. It records the booking reference, who booked, how many tickets they booked and whether they have paid. Festival staff use it to check tickets when people arrive.

What does “exported” mean?

Exporting means saving a copy of the list as a separate file, such as a spreadsheet. A helper might do this to prepare a list for staff at the entrance.

The list can contain people’s booking details, so the organiser needs to check whether this copy was authorised. Your job is to use the records below to work out what happened and what still needs checking.
