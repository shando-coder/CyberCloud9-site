Teacher facilitation notes

Allow about 5 minutes to read, 8 to build the timeline, 10 to compare explanations and 7 to close the case. Pupils can retry each task independently. For discussion, ask which exhibit changed their confidence and why. Each download includes the learner code, checked answers and attempt counts. The demo has no central results register. Use demo codes and sample records when presenting it.
