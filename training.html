CyberFirst Defenders & Advanced

I specialise in delivering official CyberFirst Defenders and Advanced courses using interactive real-world labs that are based in the cloud.

CyberFirst Defenders (Ages 14-15): Gives 14-15 year olds practical skills around how to build and protect small networks and personal devices.

  • Building and protecting small networks
  • Personal device security
  • Hands-on cybersecurity fundamentals
  • Interactive cloud-based lab exercises

CyberFirst Advanced (Ages 16-17): Students will gain advanced skills in key areas of cyber security, such as digital forensics, encryption technologies, penetration testing and open-source intelligence techniques.

  • Digital forensics and investigations
  • Encryption technologies
  • Penetration testing techniques
  • Open-source intelligence (OSINT)
  • Carrying out investigations and trying to recover lost or apparently deleted data

All courses delivered through engaging, cloud-based virtual laboratories that provide real-world cybersecurity experience.
