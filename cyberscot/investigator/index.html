Signals in the Evidence

Imagine you’re helping at the Clydebeat Festival. A copy of the ticket list has been saved. The festival organiser asks you, “Can we tell who saved it?”

You’ll read a few short records, piece together what happened and decide what you can tell the organiser.

Start the investigation
What is a ticket booking list? See an example

When someone books tickets for Clydebeat, the festival’s booking system adds a row to a list. It records the booking reference, who booked, how many tickets they booked and whether they have paid. Festival staff use it to check tickets when people arrive.

What does “exported” mean?

Exporting means saving a copy of the list as a separate file, such as a spreadsheet. A helper might do this to prepare a list for staff at the entrance.

Teacher facilitation notes

Allow about 5 minutes to read, 8 to build the timeline, 10 to compare explanations and 7 to close the case. Each step shows the records pupils need. Pupils can retry a step and review earlier steps. For discussion, ask why a shared account makes it difficult to identify the person who used it. Each download includes the learner code, checked answers and attempt counts. The demo has no central results register. Use demo codes and sample records when presenting it.
